================================================================================
                             FlightRecorder v1.0
================================================================================

FlightRecorder is a flight data recording and kinematic replay mod for KSP.
It records full 3D trajectories, control inputs, staging, and part animations,
allowing you to replay and scrub flights inside the flight scene.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Requirements and Dependencies
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Harmony, you can install it here: https://github.com/KSP-CKAN/KSP-Harmony , or
  from ckan.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. Installation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Extract the contents of this zip file.
2. Copy the FlightRecorder folder into your KSP GameData directory.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. Configuration
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Open GUI: Click the toolbar button in the AppLauncher (top/bottom right) 
  or press F7.
* Record: Click Record before launch or maneuver, fly your mission, and 
  click Stop when finished.
* Replay: Click Play to watch the flight.
* Scrub: Click and drag anywhere across the telemetry graph (yellow needle) 
  to scrub forward or backward through the flight.
* Save / Load: Click Save to save the flight to disk. Click Files to view, 
  load, rename, or delete past sessions.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. Key Features
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Kinematic Replay: Aircraft and rockets follow exact recorded coordinates without
  joint wobble or physics desync (Rovers weren't tested).
* Safe Saves: Recordings are saved in PluginData. They can be renamed in-game via
the GUI too, as well as be deleted.
