================================================================================
                             FlightRecorder v1.0
================================================================================   

This mod allows you to record your flight's telemetry data and state precisely so
you can play it as a kinematic:

- After recording your flight, and playing it, your vessel will spawn at where it 
was initially in the record, and then display the kinematic playback. 
- You can go through any time of the recording from the UI by moving the yellow 
needle through the recording.
- You can save, rename, and delete recordings. 
- Flight data is shown in a telemetry with speed, altitude and G-force data upon 
selecting a recording.
